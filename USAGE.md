# Using the workshop

Sign in at `/login` with `cenius@cenius.ai` / `cenius` (the credential is printed on the sign-in
card, and the "Sign in as the demo wheelwright" button posts it for you). Every workshop page
redirects to sign-in when you are signed out; the header shows who is signed in and carries the
sign-out control.

## Record a cartwheel build

1. Open **Cartwheel builds** in the left rail, then **New build** in the page header.
2. Enter a reference (for example `CW-1053`), pick a heritage client if the wheel belongs to a
   commission, and enter the felloe count (4–20), spoke count (8–24) and diameter in inches.
3. The guidance panel beside the form notes the workshop's typical ranges: 12–14 felloes, 12–16
   spokes.
4. **Save build** lands you on the new build's detail page, and the build appears in the list and in
   the overview's recent builds.

A wrong count returns the form with a message under that field and everything you typed still in
place. The reference must be unique.

## Edit, progress or remove a build

On a build's detail page the right-hand panel edits every field, including status
(*In build* → *Awaiting tyre* → *Complete*). Status changes show immediately on the build page and
in the list, where complete builds are muted. Removing a build is behind a confirmation and takes
its iron tyre fittings with it.

## Book an iron tyre fitting

1. Open **Fitting schedule**. The month grid shows every booked fitting in its date cell with the
   start time and the wheel reference; today is outlined.
2. Use **← Prev / Next → / Today** to move between months.
3. In the **Book an iron tyre fitting** panel choose a wheel, a date and a start time (the workshop
   works hourly slots from 08:00 to 17:00).
4. **Book fitting** drops it into that date cell. If the slot is already held, the schedule comes
   back naming the slot and the wheel that has it, and nothing is booked.

Each fitting in the "Fittings this month" list carries **Complete** and **Cancel**. Completing a
fitting removes it from today's list on the overview; cancelling it takes it off the upcoming
fittings count.

## Keep the heritage register

**Heritage clients** lists every commission client with their contact details and how many wheels
are commissioned for them. The add panel on the right takes a name (required), email, phone and the
heritage brief.

Open a client to see the brief and every wheel commissioned for them, each linking to its build
page. The right-hand panel edits the details; saving updates the client page and the register. A
client with no builds offers a link that starts a build already linked to them.

## The overview

The home page answers "where are we today": open builds, upcoming fittings and heritage clients,
today's fittings with times and wheel references, and the six most recent builds. On an empty
workshop it invites you to record the first cartwheel build instead.
